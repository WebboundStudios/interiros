# Vision

This website should feel like walking into a luxury interior design showroom.

Every interaction should feel intentional.

Use whitespace generously.

Prioritize typography over decoration.

Images should be the hero.

Animations should be slow, smooth, and elegant.

The website should evoke sophistication, trust, craftsmanship, and exclusivity.

Avoid trends that age quickly.

Design for timelessness rather than novelty.

The final result should feel worthy of award galleries like Awwwards while remaining highly usable and performant.

Every design decision should answer one question:

"Would a luxury interior design client trust this brand more because of this?"
